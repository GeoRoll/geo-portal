## What's included

Your monthly subscription includes:

- **A public GeoRoll profile** for your business, written in plain language with structured data so people and AI search tools can understand what you do and who you serve.
- **Search engine notification**: we tell search engines about your profile when it's published or updated.
- **Listings**: we set up or improve your business listings on trusted sites, such as Google Business Profile, Bing Places, and relevant directories. Some sites send the owner a verification code; you'll just need to forward it to us.
- **Monthly AI visibility checks**: we ask AI search tools the questions your customers ask and show you how often your business is mentioned, in a private report.
- **Optional website help**: if you ask, we add structured data and AI-crawler settings to your website, using temporary access that you grant and can remove at any time. We won't change your design, products, or prices.
